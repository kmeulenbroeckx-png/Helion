Questions clients ask

What is a retail audit?

A retail audit checks whether what was agreed with retailers is really there in the store: merchandising, point-of-sale material, prices and product availability.

What does a Helion Research retail audit check?

Three areas: merchandising and trade marketing (planograms, POS, secondary placement), price (your own prices, retailer compliance, competitor prices, promotions) and availability (out-of-stock, range, distribution).

How are audit findings backed up?

Every finding is backed by photos. Computer vision helps check shelf and display audits.
